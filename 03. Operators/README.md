# 🧮 Operators in Python

Welcome to the **Operators in Python** section of the Python Learning Repository! This folder provides a comprehensive guide to Python's operators, their functionality, and practical examples to help you understand how to use them effectively in your programs. Whether you're a beginner or refreshing your knowledge, this resource will strengthen your understanding of Python's operator system.

---

## 📚 Content Overview

This section covers the following types of operators in Python:

### 1. Arithmetic Operators
- Addition (`+`): `a + b`
- Subtraction (`-`): `a - b`
- Multiplication (`*`): `a * b`
- Division (`/`): `a / b`
- Floor Division (`//`): `a // b`
- Modulus (`%`): `a % b`
- Exponentiation (`**`): `a ** b`

### 2. Comparison Operators
- Equal to (`==`): `a == b`
- Not equal to (`!=`): `a != b`
- Greater than (`>`): `a > b`
- Less than (`<`): `a < b`
- Greater than or equal to (`>=`): `a >= b`
- Less than or equal to (`<=`): `a <= b`

### 3. Logical Operators
- And (`and`): `a and b`
- Or (`or`): `a or b`
- Not (`not`): `not a`

### 4. Assignment Operators
- Assign (`=`): `a = b`
- Add and assign (`+=`): `a += b`
- Subtract and assign (`-=`): `a -= b`
- Multiply and assign (`*=`): `a *= b`
- Divide and assign (`/=`): `a /= b`
- Floor divide and assign (`//=`): `a //= b`
- Modulus and assign (`%=`): `a %= b`
- Exponent and assign (`**=`): `a **= b`

### 5. Bitwise Operators
- AND (`&`): `a & b`
- OR (`|`): `a | b`
- XOR (`^`): `a ^ b`
- NOT (`~`): `~a`
- Left shift (`<<`): `a << b`
- Right shift (`>>`): `a >> b`

### 6. Membership Operators
- In (`in`): `a in b`
- Not in (`not in`): `a not in b`

### 7. Identity Operators
- Is (`is`): `a is b`
- Is not (`is not`): `a is not b`
